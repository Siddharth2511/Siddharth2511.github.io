Research

My current work is calibrating networks of phone cameras from the flight log of the drone they track: where each camera is, which way it points, and which flights teach the most. A paper on the offline work is in preparation; online estimation with PTZ cameras comes next.

Interests: perception-driven autonomy · state estimation · UAV systems · simulation-based robotics
